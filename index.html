How to play & table rules

Make the best five-card hand using any combination of your two private cards and the five community cards. Betting happens before the flop, on the flop (3 cards), turn (1 card), and river (1 card). Check when nothing is owed, call to match, raise to increase the bet, or fold to leave the hand.

Each friend starts with 1,000 pretend chips. Blinds: 10 / 20. Fixed-limit bets: 20 before/on the flop, 40 on the turn/river, with up to 3 bets or raises per round after the blinds. Short calls go all-in; side pots and tied hands are split correctly. The dealer rotates; friends without chips sit out. Fresh table restores everyone’s chips after a hand.

Hand order: high card → pair → two pair → three of a kind → straight → flush → full house → four of a kind → straight flush → royal flush. An ace can be high or low in a straight. Bots use their own cards and the shared board. Your table pauses when you leave; reloading starts a fresh table.
